# Example persona — Alex Chen

A fictional persona used to populate the example output in this repo. Replace with your own subject when you fork.

## Profile

**Name:** Alex Chen (fictional)
**Role:** Senior individual contributor, ML engineering team at a mid-size transportation logistics company
**Level:** Senior (aiming at Staff in the next review cycle)
**Tenure:** 4 years at current company, 8 years total
**Location:** San Francisco

## Current work

**Flagship project:** An internal agentic workflow that lets non-engineers query a routing-decisions data warehouse in natural language. Alex is the TL. Sprint-committed deliverable in 6 weeks.

**Secondary work:** Ongoing A/B test design and readout for driver-app experiments. Approximately 1–2 experiments per month.

**Mentorship:** Loose mentoring of 2 junior data scientists. Not a formal manager relationship.

## Time and bandwidth

Works 6 days a week during crunch periods, 5.5 on average. Partner works in a demanding role too — shared time is scarce. Evenings are the only reliable "ingest" window.

## Political context

Team is mid-reorg. Leadership is split between (a) an AI-first direction (pushed by the new VP), and (b) a "finish the current platform rebuild first" camp (pushed by the incumbent director). Alex's project sits at the intersection — visible upside on both sides, but also the kind of project that gets used as a political football.

## AI tool stack

- Primary: Claude for most work (writing, code review, experiment design)
- Secondary: ChatGPT for image generation and occasional cross-check on complex questions
- Pattern: Does not trust a single model for high-stakes design work; routinely runs a second model over output for verification

## External scanning habits (pre-vault)

- Subscribes to Simon Willison's newsletter, Latent Space, Eugene Yan — reads when bandwidth allows (inconsistent)
- Follows a dozen ML accounts on X but rarely opens the app
- GitHub trending: checks weekly when not crunching, skips during sprint crunches

## What Alex wants from the vault

- Surface tools and patterns that reduce current-work effort (experiment design, agentic SQL, SQL evals) — these compound
- Flag concepts too load-bearing to skip even at cost (agent eval frameworks, post-training patterns, anything that directly affects the flagship project)
- Skip the noise — don't notify for "interesting but not applicable"
- Build promo-receipts over time (source → wiki → idea → project → ship)

## Why this persona shape generalizes

The profile above is deliberately representative of a common shape: a senior IC at a non-FAANG tech company, in a role where AI is reshaping the day job, with scarce bandwidth and active political context. The vault's filter is designed to be most useful for someone in that shape — senior enough that routine information is already handled, bandwidth-constrained enough that noise is expensive, and in a role with enough political context that legibility matters as much as output.

If your subject is at a very different shape (e.g., an early-career IC who needs more hand-holding, or a manager whose bandwidth is eaten by people-work rather than technical work), the bandwidth filter still applies but the page templates probably need adaptation.
