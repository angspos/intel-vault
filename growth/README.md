# growth/

Recurring themes surfaced from the chat archive and from lint passes. These are patterns, not single sources.

A `growth/` page exists when the same topic has shown up multiple times in (a) the subject's conversations, (b) external sources that have been ingested, or (c) the subject's explicit areas of career focus. The page documents the theme, cites the signals, and names a concrete bridge (reading list, tool, habit, or idea) that would close the gap.

## When to create a growth/ page vs. a wiki/ page

- `wiki/` — a single concept, tool, workflow, or person page. Unit: one thing.
- `growth/` — a theme that spans multiple wiki pages. Unit: a pattern.

A `growth/` page typically links out to 3–10 wiki pages and/or raw sources.

## Weekly digests also live here

The weekly digest artifact (`growth/weekly-YYYY-MM-DD.md`) is a specific flavor of growth entry — it aggregates the current state of the vault into a concise weekly output. See `examples/weekly-digest.md` for the shape.

## Discipline

A `growth/` page must:
- Lead with a verdict (like wiki pages)
- Name at least one concrete bridge (reading, tool, action)
- Cite the signals (archive conversation counts, role shape, specific raw sources)
- Carry the same Subject-Angle frontmatter fields so it filters through the weekly digest correctly
