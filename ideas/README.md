# ideas/

Candidate projects — things the vault has surfaced that the subject *could* build or pilot but hasn't committed to yet. Ranked by role-fit (`priority: high | med | low`) and linked to the originating source.

Each idea is an attempt. Most will get shelved. The ones that don't graduate into `projects/` when the subject starts actively building.

## Template

See `CLAUDE.md` — `### ideas/ entry` section.

Key fields:
- `status`: candidate / piloting / shelved
- `priority`: high / med / low
- `origin`: the wiki page or raw source this idea came from
- "Smallest next step" section — the lightest action that would either validate or kill the idea

## Discipline

Every idea must have a concrete "smallest next step." An idea without a next step is aspiration, not a candidate. Shelve it with a reason instead of keeping it open indefinitely.
